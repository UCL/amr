Figure FA1 — Infection vs. Carriage Ecology Across 42 Organisms

Target year: 2025  |  Window: 4.00 simulated years (totals annualized to yearly equivalents)  |  Simulated population (mean): 61,265  |  Population scale factor: 133,844.8670  |  Runs: 1 accepted calibration run

Each point is one organism; bubble area is proportional to simulated annual infection deaths. Colour: red = infection >> carriage (obligate/invasive pathogens); green = carriage >> infection (mainly commensals). Both axes are symmetric log scale.

[Download SVG]

  1. Points above the dashed diagonal have higher infection than carriage prevalence, characteristic of invasive obligate pathogens (e.g. Neisseria gonorrhoeae, Neisseria meningitidis, Mycobacterium tuberculosis).
  2. Points below the diagonal have higher carriage than infection prevalence, characteristic of opportunistic commensals (e.g. E. coli, S. epidermidis, Bacteroides fragilis).
  3. Bubble area is proportional to simulated infection deaths. Organisms with zero deaths are shown at a fixed minimum size.
  4. This figure is not derived from an existing table; it provides a cross-organism ecological overview of the simulated pathogen landscape.
