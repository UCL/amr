Figure F2 — Infection Resistance Calibration Fit by Organism

Target year: 2025  |  Window: 4.00 simulated years (totals annualized to yearly equivalents)  |  Simulated population (mean): 585,649  |  Population scale factor: 14,001.5687  |  Runs: 2 accepted calibration runs

Each panel shows the simulated (blue) and surveillance-target (orange) infection resistance percentage by drug class for one priority organism. Simulation bars show the mean across all drugs in the class; error bars span the 5th–95th percentile range across 2 accepted runs. Classes without data for a given organism are omitted.

  1. Drug class resistance within a panel is averaged across all specific drugs in that class.
  2. Drugs flagged as negligible potency (baseline potency < 0.15) are excluded from class averages.
  3. All organisms with resistance benchmark data in the simulation output are included. IHME/WHO-ESKAPE priority organisms are shown first, remainder alphabetically.
