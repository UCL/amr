  1. Supplementary Table S1 summarises baseline-policy infection outcomes by bacterium in the 2022-2025 window. Counts are raw simulated counts unless otherwise stated. Rates are calculated from summed numerators and denominators over the window within each run before cross-run summarisation.
  2. Treatment exposure is counted while a person is infected with the bacterium and may include empiric therapy, targeted therapy, combination therapy, and bystander antibiotic exposure from other concurrent infections.
  3. Effective treated infection-days use the same resistance-adjusted activity threshold as Figure 10 / the sepsis effective-therapy summary: activity_r >= 0.500.
  4. Infection resolutions count immune-clearance plus drug-assisted-clearance resolution records, excluding death as the episode-ending event.
  5. Sepsis onsets use the same per-bacterium incident sepsis semantics as the sepsis effective-therapy summary: a bacterium contributes on the timestep its sepsis episode begins while the infection is active.
  6. Deaths are counted per pathogen involved. In polymicrobial cases, the same death can appear under more than one bacterium, so the sum across bacteria can exceed the headline infection-death total.
  7. Drug / treatment failure events are the model's existing day-5 post-drug-initiation failure events, aggregated to bacterium. They are not inferred from ineffective treated infection-days.
  8. Reliability flags mark rows with small denominators; rates in those rows should be interpreted cautiously.
  9. Source: existing simulation_summary_run#.csv aggregate columns only. No event-level CSV is required or produced.
  10. Run/window: Target year: 2025; window: 4.00 simulated years (totals annualized to yearly equivalents); simulated population (mean): 1,746,967; population scale factor: 4,693.8503; runs: 5 accepted calibration runs.
  11. Intervals are omitted to keep the supplementary table readable; displayed values are medians.
