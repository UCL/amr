  2. Burgundy error bars show two-sided 95% t confidence intervals for simulation means across 6 independent stochastic runs. Bacteria for which both values are zero are omitted; rows are sorted by the review-informed calibration target where available, otherwise by the simulation.
  3. Bacterium-specific death targets use the GBD 2019 central estimates and published 95% uncertainty intervals where model and GBD definitions align. Broader GBD groups are allocated or used as proxies where taxonomic definitions differ; organisms not reported in the GBD 33-pathogen analysis retain explicitly labelled review-informed estimates.
  4. These are calibration benchmarks for 2025 model output based primarily on 2019 burden estimates. Bacterium-level model outputs can count a polymicrobial death against more than one organism and therefore should not be summed to reconstruct the person-level 7.7 million headline target. H. pylori and MDR-TB are reported separately and excluded from that headline comparison.
