  3. Supplementary Figure S1 estimates the potential treatment-option landscape, not realised prescribing. For each active infection, the numerator sums baseline potency x resistance-adjusted retained activity across drugs that existed at that time and had non-negligible baseline potency against the bacterium. The denominator sums the same baseline potencies without resistance. The metric therefore estimates the fraction of potential activity retained after resistance across available modelled drugs.

  7. Annual percentages are calculated by summing numerator and denominator first within each run-year, then dividing. Panel A shaded band shows 5th-95th percentile across runs. Runs: 3.
