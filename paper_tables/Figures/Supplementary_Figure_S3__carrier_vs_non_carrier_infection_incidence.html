  1. Run/window: Target year: 2025; window: 4.00 simulated years (totals annualized to yearly equivalents); simulated population (mean): 5,819,372; population scale factor: 1,409.0867; runs: 1 accepted calibration run.
  2. Baseline-policy carrier versus non-carrier infection incidence using simulation_summary_*.csv aggregate fields.
  3. Carrier status is assessed before infection acquisition in each daily timestep. Carriers are people with microbiome/carriage presence for the bacterium and no active infection with that bacterium at the start of the timestep. Non-carriers have no microbiome/carriage presence and no active infection with that bacterium. Incident events are successful infection acquisitions occurring later in that timestep.
  4. Rates use at-risk person-years as the denominator. People already actively infected with the bacterium are excluded from both carrier and non-carrier denominators for that bacterium.
  5. This figure is a structural diagnostic of the carriage-to-infection pathway. It should not be interpreted as an external epidemiological estimate unless separately calibrated.
  6. Rates and rate ratios are calculated within each run from summed 2022-2025 baseline-policy numerators and denominators, then summarised as medians across runs.
  7. Values are raw simulated counts and rates from 1 simulation run; 5th-95th intervals are omitted to keep the paired-bacterium figure readable.
  8. Any-R incident infections are classified using the same infection-level any-R threshold used elsewhere in the simulation summaries.
