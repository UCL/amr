  1. Run/window: Target year: 2025; window: 4.00 simulated years (totals annualized to yearly equivalents); simulated population (mean): 5,830,969; population scale factor: 1,406.2844; runs: 10 accepted calibration runs.
  2. Each panel shows the simulated (burgundy) and calibration-benchmark (dark grey) infection resistance percentage by drug class for one bacterium. Simulation bars show the mean of run-level class means; error bars show a two-sided 95% t confidence interval across stochastic runs. Classes without data for a given bacterium are omitted.
  3. Where shown, simulation error bars are two-sided 95% t confidence intervals for the mean across independent stochastic runs. They quantify run-to-run Monte Carlo uncertainty conditional on the chosen parameter set and model structure; they do not represent uncertainty in parameters, calibration targets, or model structure. No confidence interval can be estimated from a single run.
  4. Figure 2 dark-grey bars are point calibration benchmarks. Target uncertainty bars are not shown because cell-level source intervals have not yet been recovered; their absence should not be interpreted as certainty.
  5. Figure 2ii summary mode: mean_ci. To switch modes, edit FIGURE2_SUMMARY_MODE in make_paper_tables.py.
  6. Drug class resistance within a panel is averaged across all specific drugs in that class.
  7. Drugs flagged as negligible potency (baseline potency < 0.15) are excluded from class averages.
  8. Drug classes are shown only where at least one drug in the class has baseline potency >= 0.25 for that bacterium.
  9. Rifamycins and MDR Mycobacterium tuberculosis are omitted from this broad calibration figure because they are special-case TB/rifampicin-resistance outputs rather than comparable general drug-class calibration cells.
  10. This part uses a presentational bacterial grouping for legibility; the grouping does not define model strata. All eligible bacteria are retained across Figure 2i, Figure 2ii and Figure 2iii.
  11. This part displays enterobacterales and other enteric gram-negative bacteria.
  12. Resistance-prevalence values are evidence-informed calibration benchmarks. ECDC EARS-Net, WHO GLASS reports, and organism-drug literature informed the legacy matrix, but cell-level citations and harmonised denominator definitions were not retained.
  13. Conditional mean any_r values are expert-assigned model benchmarks on the model's unitless resistance scale; they are not MIC values or direct surveillance estimates.
  14. The versioned target set distinguishes evidence-unresolved benchmarks, expert-informed placeholders, and structural priors. No v1 cell is classified as a direct empirical estimate with a recovered cell-level source, and no evidence-quality weight is assigned.
  15. Both benchmark families compare with simulated active-infection person-days and should not be interpreted as a harmonised global clinical-isolate surveillance dataset.
