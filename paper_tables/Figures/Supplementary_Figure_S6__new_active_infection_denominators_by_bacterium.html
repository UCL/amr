  7. Counts are raw simulated n values, not population-scaled incidence estimates.
  8. Small denominators can make serious-R percentages unstable; apparent 0% or 100% serious-R values should be interpreted cautiously when denominators are sparse.
  9. Serious-R is a model-defined marker-drug resistance endpoint and is not a directly observed surveillance category.
  10. If multiple bacteria can be involved in one infection episode, bacterium-specific infection events may count separately by bacterium.
  11. Counts are from the supplied run.
  12. Denominator adequacy categories are interpretation aids only: no denominator = n = 0; very sparse = 1-19; sparse = 20-99; moderate = 100-499; larger denominator = >=500.
