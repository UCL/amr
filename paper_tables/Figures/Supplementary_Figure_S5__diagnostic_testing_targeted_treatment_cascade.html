Definitions, Denominators, and Exclusions

Item Definition
Denominator Eligible symptomatic infection episodes entering the diagnostic cascade.
Time window Baseline-policy cascade-entry years 2022-2025, using rows from simulation_summary_run#.csv.
Data source Aggregate simulation_summary_run#.csv columns only; calibration_summary_*.txt is not reader-facing for this figure.
Community/hospital split Hospital status is captured at cascade entry. Community means not hospitalized; hospital means hospitalized.
Resistance testing stage The Rust flag records resistance/susceptibility testing initiation, not confirmed result availability.
Effective targeted therapy A targeted-context active antibiotic with resistance-adjusted activity_r >= 0.500 for the bacterium.
Timing semantics All downstream stages are assigned back to the episode's cascade-entry timestep/year. The model uses daily timesteps, so same-day order is not a precise sub-day clinical timestamp.
Organism exclusions The Rust aggregate excludes organisms returned by is_microbiome_excluded (currently treponema_pallidum). H. pylori is not separately excluded by that helper in the current code.
Exclusions and caveats Counts are raw simulated counts. Episodes that resolve, die, or are censored before a later stage remain in the earlier-stage denominator and are not imputed into later stages.
  1. Run/window: Target year: 2025; window: 4.00 simulated years (totals annualized to yearly equivalents); simulated population (mean): 1,749,575; population scale factor: 4,686.8517; runs: 1 accepted calibration run.
  2. Diagnostic testing and targeted-treatment cascade from aggregate simulation summary columns.
  3. Denominator: eligible symptomatic bacterium-specific infection episodes entering the diagnostic cascade under baseline policy with cascade-entry years 2022-2025.
  4. Counts are raw simulated counts. When multiple runs are supplied, counts and percentages are calculated within each run first and shown as medians across runs.
  5. All stages after eligibility are assigned back to the episode's cascade-entry timestep/year, so the 2022-2025 filter is based on cascade-entry year.
  6. Community and hospital groups use hospital status at cascade entry; community means not hospitalized and hospital means hospitalized.
  7. The resistance-testing stage is labelled as initiation because the Rust flag records AST/resistance testing initiation, not final result availability.
  8. Effective targeted therapy means a targeted-context active antibiotic with activity_r >= 0.500 for the bacterium, matching the sepsis effective-therapy threshold.
  9. The model uses daily timesteps, so same-model-day ordering is not a precise sub-day clinical timestamp.
  10. Data source: aggregate simulation_summary_run#.csv fields only. calibration_summary_*.txt is not used as a reader-facing substitute for this figure.
  11. The Rust aggregate excludes organisms returned by is_microbiome_excluded (currently treponema_pallidum). H. pylori is not separately excluded by that helper in the current code.
  12. Values shown are medians across 1 simulation run.
