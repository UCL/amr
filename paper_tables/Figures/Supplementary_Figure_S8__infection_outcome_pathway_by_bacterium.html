Reliability flag legend

Reliability flag Meaning
low infection denominator Fewer than 100 acquisition events in the run-level median denominator.
low treated denominator Fewer than 100 treated infection-days in the run-level median denominator.
low sepsis count Fewer than 20 sepsis-onset events in the run-level median numerator.
low death count Fewer than 20 infection-death events in the run-level median numerator.
unstable rate At least one small-count flag applies; pathway rates should be interpreted cautiously.
  1. Run/window: Target year: 2025; window: 4.00 simulated years (totals annualized to yearly equivalents); simulated population (mean): 1,764,019; population scale factor: 4,648.4767; runs: 5 accepted calibration runs.
  2. Integrated pathway view of baseline-policy infection outcomes by bacterium; rows are sorted by median infection deaths, then median acquisition events.
  3. Values summarise baseline-policy infection outcomes by bacterium in the 2022-2025 window.
  4. Counts are raw simulated counts unless otherwise stated.
  5. Rates are calculated from summed run-level numerators and denominators over the window.
  6. Treatment exposure is counted while a person is infected with the bacterium and may include empiric therapy, targeted therapy, combination therapy, and bystander antibiotic exposure.
  7. Effective treated infection-days use the same resistance-adjusted activity threshold as the sepsis effective-therapy summary, expected activity_r >= 0.500.
  8. Infection resolutions count active infection episodes ending by clearance/resolution rather than death.
  9. Sepsis onsets use the existing simulation_summary aggregate semantics.
  10. Deaths are counted per pathogen involved; in polymicrobial cases, the same death can appear under more than one bacterium.
  11. Drug / treatment failure events use the existing model-defined failure-event semantics.
  12. Reliability flags mark small denominators and unstable rates.
  13. Metrics are calculated within each run first and displayed as medians across 5 accepted simulation runs.
  14. Source: existing simulation_summary_*.csv aggregate columns only. No event-level or supplementary-output CSV file is required or produced.
